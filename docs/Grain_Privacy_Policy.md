# Grain — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Grain is a habit app that helps you invest a few minutes a day in what matters to you. This policy explains what information Grain uses, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- Your pillars, daily logs, notes and streaks are stored **only on your phone**. We never see them.
- We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number or exact location, and you don't need an account.
- We collect **anonymous usage events** (for example, "a pillar was logged") so we can understand how Grain is used and improve it.
- We don't sell your data or show ads.

## Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter into Grain stays on your device. That includes your pillars, logs, notes, streaks, reminder time and settings. It is not uploaded to us or anyone else.

If you uninstall Grain, or use **Settings → Reset everything**, that information is deleted from your phone. We can't recover it, because we never had a copy.

## Anonymous usage analytics

Grain uses **Mixpanel**, an analytics service, to record how the app is used. When you use the app, it sends events such as:

- the app was opened, or a screen or tab was viewed
- onboarding was started or completed
- a pillar was selected, added, removed or logged, including the pillar's category name (for example "Learning")
- a streak milestone was reached
- the daily reminder was turned on or off, or its time was changed
- logs or the app were reset

Each event includes a **random ID created on your phone**, the app version, the platform (Android) and the time. The random ID isn't linked to your name, email or Google account.

We **don't** send your notes, the contents of your logs, or anything you type.

Our Mixpanel data is stored in the European Union. When your phone sends an event, Mixpanel receives your device's IP address, as any internet service does. Mixpanel may use it to estimate a general location such as country or city. Grain itself doesn't use your precise location.

We use this information only to understand which features help people and to fix problems.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Grain schedules it **on your phone**. We don't use a push-notification server, and the reminder doesn't send anything to us. You can turn reminders off in Grain's Settings or in your phone's notification settings at any time.

## Contacting us

If you email us (for example, through "Suggest a feature" or "Report a bug"), we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and improve Grain.

## Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. The only other company that handles Grain data is Mixpanel, which processes the anonymous usage events described above on our behalf.

## Children

Grain is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

- **Delete your on-device data:** use Settings → Reset everything, or uninstall Grain.
- **Turn off reminders:** use Settings → Daily reminder, or your phone's notification settings.
- **Questions or requests about analytics data:** email us. Usage data is linked only to a random ID, not to you, so we usually can't single out one person's data. We'll do our best to help.

## Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when Grain changes how it handles information, for example when paid features are introduced. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
