Design notes

  • One plan, one button. Yearly only ($29, about $2.42/month), so there's no plan picker. The 7-day trial is the main call to action.
  • Headline matches the trigger. The feature they tapped is shown in bold first in the list, so the paywall answers the question they just asked.
  • Clear trial terms sit right under the button: free for 7 days, then $29/year, cancel in Google Play. Google requires this disclosure.
  • Restore purchase is always visible, for reinstalls and new phones.
  • "Not now" closes it. There are no countdown timers or tricks, which fits the calm Grain tone.
  • Price shown is a placeholder. In the app, the price comes from Google Play in the user's local currency (e.g. QAR, INR).
  • Founding 100 is still to be decided. How people redeem the 3 free months (Play promo codes or a special offer) is not confirmed yet.
  • Decide: show the paywall from day 1, or only after the first week of logging?
